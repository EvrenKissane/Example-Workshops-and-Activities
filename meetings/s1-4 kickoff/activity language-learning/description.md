# **Do Quantum**: Language Learning Activity

**Purpose**  
Practice quantum computing architecture’s vocabulary and core concepts, while developing paper-reading strategies to discuss academic papers confidently and accurately.
This should help you read and extract relevant information from an academic paper and communicate their findings simply and concisely.

**Due**  
Wednesday, 30 September @ 11:59 pm

# Instructions

**Goal**
- Dissect and interpret existing research to better understand literature.
- Practice interpreting complicated ideas and reforming them into short and simple statements.
- Communicate ideas from literature accurately and precisely.

**Steps**
1. Partner up with 1 other student.
   It may help if you find a partner with a different major or background.
2. Find a paper describing a topic of your choice.
3. Carefully read the abstract, and write notes.
      + On your first pass, identify any words or phrases specific to quantum.
      + On your second pass, identify concepts and ideas specific to this project.
4. Review the paper's content, and write comments.
   Was everything in the abstract explained in the paper's body or was something missing? Why?
   Did the abstract have more or less content, in terms of concepts and ideas, than the body?
5. Re-write the abstract in two ways:
      + Layman: Keep the ideas, but do not use any words specific to quantum.
      + Concise: Word everything as concisely as possible, while maintaining the ideas. Technical words are allowed here.
   How do your rewrites compare to the original abstract?
6. **ONCE ACCEPTED** Optionally, look at accepted teams & projects to have a good idea of who else will be at the next meeting.

**Submission**
1. Navigate to the "Activity Folder."  
2. Create a folder for your partnership named: "paper topic: fun name you choose"
3. Upload your written notes and rewrites into that folder, and a folder with reference materials.  
4. Link your folder in the submission spreadsheet.
5. Be assessed as a team and get feedback by Thursday, 01 October @ 11:59 pm.

# Rubric

**Notes**
- First-pass notes are specific to the language itself (key terms, words, and phrases)
- Second-pass notes are specific to concepts and ideas
- Comparisons between abstract and paper body are justified
- Questions target assumptions, metrics, or missing details, that materially affect understanding

**Rewrites**
- Core ideas are preserved in each version
- Quantum-specific words are not present in the layman version
- Lowest word count in complex abstract rewrite
- Terminology used accurately when present

**Submission**
- Folder named correctly: "paper topic: fun name you choose."  
- Materials uploaded by deadline
- Organization, clarity, and adherence to instructions

# Tips
1. Bad papers are out there! If you find one, don't immediately switch away, unless you can't do this assignment.
2. If a system is explained, try diagramming it.
3. Look past the big picture. Usually, papers have a small scope of impact.
4. LLMs are great for simplification, but not great for losslessly condensing technical information.
5. For this activity, this process is more important than the result. Don't worry if you make mistakes your first time.
